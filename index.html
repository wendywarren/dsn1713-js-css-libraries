Lakeside Night Market

Local food, makers and music on the waterfront, 6 pm to midnight.

See the vendors Plan your visit

What you'll find

Street food

Over 30 vendors serving dumplings, tacos, bannock, gelato and more.

Local makers

Prints, ceramics, candles and clothing from artists across the region.

Live music

Two stages with local bands and DJs from 7 pm until close.

Plan your visit

The market is free to enter. It's a short walk from public transit, and parking is available at both entrances.

  • When: Every Friday, 6 pm to midnight
  • Where: The boardwalk at Lakeside Park
  • Cost: Free entry
